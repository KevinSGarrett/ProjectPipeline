# Project Closure and Archive Runbook

1. Move the project to `CLOSING` only through normal typed control authority.
2. Verify final accepted requirements and final release evidence.
3. Reconcile Jira and Git; require clean or explicitly dispositioned residual state.
4. Build the evidence archive, final Jira snapshot, architecture/handoff record, and retention manifest.
5. Verify final backup **and an isolated restore**.
6. Generate resource-release, credential-revocation, and scheduled-task-disable plans.
7. Evaluate legal holds and retained references. Retention expiry alone never authorizes deletion.
8. Submit the archive transition as a separately authorized action.
9. After archive verification, execute separately authorized cleanup actions and record evidence.
10. Keep sufficient restart/handoff state for later reactivation until decommission policy permits otherwise.
