L000009 | ## PLAN-OPS-001:SEC-01 Telemetry model
L000010 | 
L000011 | Structured logs, metrics, traces, audit events, and evidence use shared correlation identifiers across project, work, workflow, agent, provider, context, cost, incident, and external-system interactions.
L000012 | 
L000013 | ## PLAN-OPS-001:SEC-02 Health computation
L000014 | 
L000015 | Health is derived from observed state and freshness rather than a single process heartbeat. Component, project, provider, synchronization, budget, evidence, and recovery health remain independently visible.
L000016 | 
L000017 | ## PLAN-OPS-001:SEC-03 Audit
L000018 | 
L000019 | Security-sensitive and state-changing actions create append-only audit events containing actor, authority, action intent, target, policy result, outcome, correlation, and evidence references.
L000020 | 
L000021 | ## PLAN-OPS-001:SEC-04 Operational lifecycle
L000022 | 
L000023 | Operations cover installation, configuration, boot, shutdown, monitoring, recovery, upgrade, backup, restore, incident response, troubleshooting, and developer and operator onboarding.
L000024 | 
L000025 | ## PLAN-OPS-001:SEC-05 Runbook integration
L000026 | 
L000027 | Incidents link to versioned runbooks and observed evidence. Suggested remediation does not become an accepted runbook until reviewed and verified against applicable environments.
