L000009 | ## PLAN-ASSURE-001:SEC-01 Assurance controls
L000010 | 
L000011 | Execution assurance includes attempt budgets, novelty and progress checks, scope control, dependency-change governance, executable acceptance criteria, evidence freshness, independent review, and a final Completion Gate.
L000012 | 
L000013 | ## PLAN-ASSURE-001:SEC-02 Evidence Ledger
L000014 | 
L000015 | Evidence records stable ID, claim, requirement and criterion links, producer, method, artifact, digest, observation time, environment, result, expiry or freshness rule, and verification status. Evidence is immutable; corrections append a superseding record.
L000016 | 
L000017 | ## PLAN-ASSURE-001:SEC-03 Test portfolio
L000018 | 
L000019 | Testing is selected by risk and includes unit, component, contract, integration, API, end-to-end, property, mutation where justified, adversarial, fault, performance, security, accessibility, visual, browser, resilience, recovery, installer, upgrade, and rollback tests.
L000020 | 
L000021 | ## PLAN-ASSURE-001:SEC-04 Independence
L000022 | 
L000023 | Higher-risk work requires evidence from a reviewer or mechanism distinct from the implementation path. A worker cannot establish final truth merely by reporting its own success.
L000024 | 
L000025 | ## PLAN-ASSURE-001:SEC-05 Completion Gate
L000026 | 
L000027 | Completion is convergence among source, requirement, plan, decision, work, implementation, configuration, infrastructure, tests, evidence, documentation, deployment, and operational readiness. Any unexplained gap prevents full completion.
