L000009 | ## PLAN-CTRL-001:SEC-01 Control responsibilities
L000010 | 
L000011 | The control plane owns canonical project state, requirement acceptance, work eligibility, dependency truth, scope boundaries, authorization, policy evaluation, completion recomputation, and reconciliation after change.
L000012 | 
L000013 | ## PLAN-CTRL-001:SEC-02 Authority separation
L000014 | 
L000015 | - Project Control Kernel: deterministic state and transition authority.
L000016 | - Autonomy Director: portfolio and project coordination.
L000017 | - Architecture Authority: bounded architectural proposals and decision preparation.
L000018 | - Review Authority: independent findings and verification disposition.
L000019 | - Completion Authority: evidence-backed final gate.
L000020 | - Jira and Repository Stewards: synchronized external-system state within policy.
L000021 | 
L000022 | No role may self-approve high-risk work when separation is required.
L000023 | 
L000024 | ## PLAN-CTRL-001:SEC-03 State transitions
L000025 | 
L000026 | Transitions require a valid source state, authorized actor or policy, satisfied preconditions, idempotency identity, and an audit event. Unknown outcomes enter reconciliation instead of being retried blindly.
L000027 | 
L000028 | ## PLAN-CTRL-001:SEC-04 Durable execution
L000029 | 
L000030 | Long-running actions require persistent workflow identity, checkpoints, retry classification, idempotency keys, transactional boundaries, outbox/inbox handling where needed, and recovery after worker or control-process loss.
L000031 | 
L000032 | ## PLAN-CTRL-001:SEC-05 Recommendation boundary
L000033 | 
L000034 | Models may recommend sequencing, context, risk, or remediation. Deterministic validators decide whether the recommendation is admissible. Plan conflicts are recorded and rejected or escalated; they are never silently resolved by model preference.
